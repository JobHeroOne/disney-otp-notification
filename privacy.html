Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Purpose

Disney OTP SMS is a private automation that processes selected Gmail messages to forward verification codes via SMS.

Data accessed

The application requests read-only Gmail access. It processes relevant email metadata and message content to identify verification codes.

Data sharing

Extracted verification codes and recipient phone numbers are sent to Bird for SMS delivery.

Storage and security

Authentication credentials are intended to be stored securely in Google Cloud. Verification codes are not intended to be retained after processing. Operational metadata may be retained to prevent duplicate delivery.

Access and control

The Google account owner can revoke the application's Gmail access through Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner using the support email shown on the OAuth consent screen.
